VEX-Rank Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Back to vex-rank.com

The short version: you can use VEX-Rank without an account, and then nothing about you leaves your browser. If you sign in with Discord, we keep your Discord user id, display name and avatar, plus the scouting data you choose to save. We don't sell anything, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.

Without an account

The site saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it works the way you left it: your colour theme, Paka the cat (name, coat, mood, position), and your scouting data (favourite teams and events, "my team", match notes, picklists and livestream links for the Jumper). It stays on your device; clear your site data to remove it. We use no cookies.

If you tick Near me on the events page, your browser asks for your location. It is used only in your browser to measure distances to events and is never sent to us.

Signing in with Discord

Sign-in is optional. It uses Discord's identify permission only, so we receive your Discord user id, display name and avatar. We do not receive your email address, your servers or your messages.

  • Your scouting data is saved to your account so it follows you between devices.
  • Scouting groups: picklists and match notes you add to a group can be read and edited by everyone in that group, and they can see your name and avatar as a member and as the author of notes and changes. Anyone with a group's invite code can join it; the group's owner can change the code.
  • Votes: totals on teams and events are public; who voted which way is not shown to anyone.
  • Sign-in keeps a session token in your browser for 30 days. We store only a one-way hash of it.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open your account menu and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, your synced data, your votes and your group memberships (groups you own pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the only one). Notes you wrote in a group remain for the group unless you delete them first. Signing out alone keeps your account.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and Cloudflare runs our API and database. Like any web host they see your IP address and request details when you connect, and may keep them briefly in standard logs.
  • Discord handles sign-in when you choose it.
  • YouTube plays livestreams on the Jumper page, through its privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) mode, only when you load a video.
  • Team, event and match data comes from the public events.vex.com API.

Children

Many VEX competitors are students. VEX-Rank works fully without an account, and signing in collects only what Discord's identify permission provides.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we'll update this page and its effective date first. Questions: ask in the VEX-Rank Discord server.
