## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-01

The first numbered release: the site as it is live today.

### Rankings
- World team ranking from the VEX Competitive Rating (VCR), recalculated live
  from official match results, with a ± range showing how settled each rating is.
- All teams, High School and Middle School tabs; filters by season, country
  and event region; team search.

### Events
- Event calendar for the season with filters for region, type, format, grade,
  date range, World qualifiers and more.
- Event pages with general info, agenda, travel info, webcast, awards and the
  registered team list.
- Results: qualification standings, eliminations in a classic or a bracket
  view, and skills rankings.

### Teams
- Team profiles with the team number drawn stroke by stroke, season history
  and links to the official team page.
- A team's tournament page: where they stand, a strength hexagon (OPR, CCWM,
  DPR, autonomous win points, win rate, skills) against the rest of the
  division, a match-by-match momentum chart, and every match played and to come.

### Paka
- Paka, a pixel-art cat that lives in the corner on desktop: click it for a
  random trick, stroke it to make it purr, triple-click for zoomies, and drag
  it anywhere. Ask Paka (the speech-bubble button) finds teams, compares two
  teams, lists the top teams in a place or the next events, and follows teams
  to tell you when they have played new matches.

### Site
- VEX-Rank name and icon, four colour themes (Midnight, Ember, Abyss, Moss),
  a Discord link, and shareable links to any team, event or tournament page.
- A footer disclaimer: VEX-Rank is not affiliated with VEX Robotics and is not
  a VEX product.
